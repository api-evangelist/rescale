Develop aerodynamic AI Physics models with HiLiftAeroML, an open-source dataset now available natively within Rescale AI Physics. Built from surface-based simulation data across four geometries and multiple angles of attack, it gives engineers a practical starting point for training AI surrogate models without first assembling and building their own dataset.
