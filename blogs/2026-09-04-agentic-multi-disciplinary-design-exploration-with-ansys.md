Connect CFD insight to downstream engineering decisions with an agentic Ansys CFX and Mechanical workflow on Rescale. The workflow combines the power of agentic engineering with deterministic simulation across multiple disciplines, analyzing a completed CFX design study, identifying key parameters and data gaps, kicking off follow-on studies, and then brings CFX pressure loads into Mechanical so hydraulic and structural results can be evaluated together.
